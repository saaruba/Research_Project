# References — Final Consolidated Draft (for the dissertation's References chapter)

**Written 26 August 2026, replacing the stale 21 August version entirely.** This is the master reference list for the whole dissertation, built by extracting the actual reference list from each of the five chapters that carries one (Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results & Discussion, Conclusion — Chapter 4/Implementation deliberately carries none, per its "not for the report" brief), merging them into one alphabetical list, and removing duplicates. No new sources are introduced here — every entry below was already verified and used in at least one chapter.

**Whole-dissertation audit performed before this list was finalised**, exactly as done for every individual chapter this session: every chapter's own reference list was extracted, merged, and de-duplicated by surname+year; then the combined body text of all six chapters (Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Implementation, Results & Discussion, Conclusion — Implementation has no reference list but was still checked for stray citations) was grepped against the merged list in both directions. Result: **36 unique sources**, every one confirmed cited in-text somewhere in the dissertation, and no citation-looking pattern anywhere in the six chapters' combined body text that is missing from this list. Two names that matched the citation-pattern search — "Faris et al., 2025" and "Gao et al., 2019" — are not orphans: they appear only inside the Literature Review's own verification note, which explicitly states both were investigated and *excluded* as unverifiable in an earlier proposal draft and remain excluded here. No year conflicts were found between chapters — every source that appears in more than one chapter (e.g. Hall, 1966; Kendon, 1990; Vascon *et al.*, 2016; Fisher, 1922; Ríos-Martínez, Spalanzani and Laugier, 2015) carries the identical year and identical full citation everywhere it appears, including Repiso, Garrell and Sanfeliu, which is correctly (2020) throughout after last session's fix.

Formatted in Harvard style, matching the citation style used consistently across all six chapters. Alphabetical by first author surname.
